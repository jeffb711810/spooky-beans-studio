They hang a chair high up a wall... so nobody can sit in it again.
You see, in 1702, Thomas Busby and his father-in-law make fake coins. One night, Busby finds him sitting in his chair. "Get out of my chair." They fight. Busby kills him. Before he hangs, he curses it: whoever sits in that chair... dies.
For two hundred years, it sits in a village pub, and the locals think it's hilarious. "Go on, sit in the dead man's chair!"
Then something weird happens... allegedly. In the war, bomber crews dare each other to sit in it... and never come home. A chimney sweep sits for one drink. Next morning, he falls off a roof. Two pilots sit in it for a laugh. On the drive home... crash. And the chair just sits there. Waiting.
The jokes stop. The landlord drags it to the museum. "Put it where nobody can sit."
An expert checks the wood... and that chair was built a hundred years after Busby died.
