# The Chair Nobody Is Allowed to Sit In: shot plan

Voice: `voice/voice_tight.wav` (60.1 s; Aiden take 2, pauses tightened, tempo 1.05). Word times are in `voice/words.json`.
The voice starts at f6 (0.2 s). The film is 1828 f (60.9 s). The last line runs straight back into the first, so the film loops.

The quality bar is the look-dev chair (`hero.ts`), and every set is built that way:
- modelled parts with bevels
- painted detail
- readable story text
- metal that shines
- dense set dressing
- no plain surfaces

The camera follows the reference moves:
- macro inserts
- huge foreground framing
- top-down shots
- flying through objects
- match cuts on the chair
- dissolves for time jumps
- low hero angles
- lateral trucks

## Builds

### Sets (dioramas)
1. Museum gallery (built). Still to add:
   - ladder and display cases
   - extra exhibits
   - a guard post
2. 1702 cottage:
   - forgers' bench (built)
   - hearth
   - door and window
   - beams
3. Inn exterior and road at dusk, with the gallows hill silhouette far off.
4. The pub (main set). Its eras switch with toggles:
   - bar counter with pumps, bottles and tankards
   - dartboard and fireplace
   - beams, windows, tables and stools
5. Airfield at night: runway, bomber silhouettes, moon.
6. Village rooftops in the morning, with chimney pots.
7. Night road: a tree and a small sports car.
8. 1978 street with the museum door.

### Music
- A catchy whistle-able main tune: pizzicato, glockenspiel and a bouncing tuba.
- A 4-note curse motif every time someone sits.
- The music stops dead for shots 10, 14 and 15.
- SFX for every action.
